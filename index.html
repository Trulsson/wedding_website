Travel & accommodation

Early January is a busy travel period. We recommend booking your journey and accommodation as soon as possible.

Arriving in Helsingborg

Copenhagen Airport is approximately 90 minutes away by train. Trains continue across the Øresund Bridge to Helsingborg via Malmö.

Where to stay

There are affordable hotels near the Reception. Let us know if you need accommodation, and we may be able to arrange a group discount.

Swedish winter

January is usually cold, with short daylight hours and possible rain, wind, snow or ice. Bring a warm coat and footwear suitable for winter streets.
